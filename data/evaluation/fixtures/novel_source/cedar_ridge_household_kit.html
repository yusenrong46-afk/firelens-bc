Cedar Ridge Household Kit Supplement

Publisher: Cedar Ridge Preparedness Office. This is a reviewed stable-guidance supplement for evaluation only.

Unique kit requirement

Every Cedar Ridge household grab-and-go bag must include one sealed amber signal whistle labeled CR-WHISTLE-9 and two blue electrolyte pouches marked CR-HYDRO-2.

Assembly rule

Place the amber signal whistle in the exterior mesh pocket and store the blue electrolyte pouches beside bottled water. Do not substitute a generic sports whistle for the amber signal whistle.

Review note

This supplement does not provide live wildfire status, evacuation orders, or personalized medical advice.
